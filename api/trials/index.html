    Trials¶

    matchminer_ai.trials turns trial documents into cohorts: the groups of people a trial enrolls, each with its complete eligibility criteria, split into key and boilerplate criteria and tagged line by line. How matching works explains how the later steps use them. The outputs are research abstractions for human review, not eligibility determinations.

    The trial steps¶

    Step Function Adds
    1. Screen for cancer treatment extract_cohorts (ctgov listings) or extract_protocol_cohorts (full protocols) Rejected trials get no rows
    2. List the cohorts and extract each one's complete criteria the same extractor cohort_name, cohort_eligibility_criteria
    3. Merge cohorts whose criteria are equivalent merge_equivalent_cohorts merged_from
    4. Tag every criteria line inclusion or exclusion, and key or boilerplate remove_boilerplate_criteria cohort_key_criteria, cohort_boilerplate_criteria, cohort_criteria_items

    process_trials runs all four, in this order:

    import pandas as pd
from matchminer_ai import load_preset
from matchminer_ai.trials import process_trials

config = load_preset("default")

listings = pd.DataFrame([{
    "trial_id": "NCT01234567",
    "trial_title": "A Phase 2 Study of Drug X in Advanced Solid Tumors",
    "brief_summary": "This study tests drug X in two cohorts ...",
    "eligibility_criteria": "Inclusion Criteria:\n- ...\nExclusion Criteria:\n- ...",
}])
cohorts, metadata = process_trials(listings, config=config, output_dir="runs/trials", return_metadata=True)

protocols = pd.DataFrame([{"trial_id": "NCT01234567", "protocol_path": "protocols/NCT01234567.pdf"}])
protocol_cohorts = process_trials(protocols, source="protocol", config=config, output_dir="runs/protocols")
    • source: "ctgov" (the default) reads ClinicalTrials.gov listings with extract_cohorts and uses the trial settings for every step. "protocol" reads full protocols with extract_protocol_cohorts and uses the protocol_cohort_extraction settings for every step; text_output_dir and progress_callback apply only here.
    • Result: one row per merged cohort of each accepted trial, with all the columns below. The extractor's decisions stay in cohorts.attrs.
    • Metadata: with return_metadata=True, each step's metadata under extraction, merge and boilerplate. Rejected trials are in metadata["extraction"]["trial_rejections"].
    • output_dir: saves per-trial results and reuses them; see saved results.

    The steps are also public functions, so a caller can run some and skip others. Without step 4, TrialSpace and the decision model's match questions read cohort_eligibility_criteria (all the criteria), while exclusion_criteria_check and check_eligibility_criteria need its columns. The merge and boilerplate steps take section="trial" (listings, the default) or section="protocol_cohort_extraction" (protocols) for their settings.

    Inputs¶

    ctgov listings (extract_cohorts): one row per trial with a unique, nonempty trial_id and the text columns trial_title, brief_summary and eligibility_criteria (strings or missing; each trial needs some text). Each listing becomes one document, Title: ..., Brief summary: and Eligibility criteria:, with the source formatting kept. ctgov_records fetches a listing by NCT ID.

    Full protocols (extract_protocol_cohorts): one row per trial with a unique trial_id and a protocol_path to a PDF or a UTF-8 .txt or .md file.

    • PDFs are converted locally with ocr_pdf. Pass text_output_dir to keep the text; otherwise it goes to a temporary directory that is removed.
    • Length: each request carries the whole document. A document longer than protocol_cohort_extraction.max_document_characters (800,000) fails; it is never truncated.
    • Privacy: the whole document reaches the configured LLM endpoint. Use documents without patient data unless the endpoint is authorized for their sensitivity.

    Cohort extraction¶

    Both extractors send each trial document through the same three prompts (matchminer_ai.trials.cohort_workflow):

    1. Screen (trial.cohorts.screen.user.txt), answered {"cancer_treatment_trial": "yes"} or "no". Yes means the trial evaluates a direct anti-cancer treatment in people with cancer. That includes adjuvant, maintenance and recurrence-prevention treatment, comparisons of treatment strategies, and studies whose only endpoints are PK, PD, safety, dose or other surrogate endpoints. Diagnostic, screening, supportive-care, toxicity-prevention, survivorship, primary-prevention and non-cancer trials are no, as are anti-cancer drugs given to people without cancer. A "no" ends the trial.
    2. Cohort names (trial.cohorts.list.user.txt), answered {"cohorts": ["Part A, dose escalation", ...]}: the parts, phases, arms or groups the trial enrolls, at the lowest level of any nesting, in document order.
      • A group is a cohort even without its own eligibility section: regimens given to separate groups, parts the summary names or the numbering implies, cohorts the criteria name. Closed cohorts are kept.
      • Not cohorts: stratification factors; crossover, extension, retreatment and rollover phases; optional sub-studies; cohorts limited to countries that exclude the United States.
      • A name only identifies a group and never narrows it. Examples given with "such as" keep that wording or are left out.
    3. Criteria, one request per cohort (trial.cohorts.criteria.user.txt), answered {"eligibility_criteria": "Inclusion criteria:\n...\nExclusion criteria:\n..."}: the cohort's complete enrollment criteria. The request lists every cohort name and ends with the target cohort.

    The criteria prompt's rules:

    • Complete: every criterion that applies to the cohort (stated for every participant, for a part or group that contains it, or for a disease it can include) and none that applies only to other cohorts. Boilerplate is copied too: consent, compliance, samples and biopsies, measurable disease, contraception, pregnancy and lactation, vaccines, study-staff and investigator-judgment exclusions. Step 4 separates it.
    • Verbatim: wording, numbers, units, operators and negation are copied. The only changes: lines split by PDF conversion are rejoined, lead-ins that only say where a criterion applies ("For Part 2 only:") are removed, and references ("see Section 5.1", "criterion 9") are replaced by their content. Qualifiers that limit a criterion to some people are kept.
    • Defined by the document: what defines the cohort (disease, histology, biomarker, prior therapy, age, sex) is stated as separate criteria, taken from the document and never from the cohort's name. Features given only as examples are not requirements, and a document criterion is never rewritten to fit the cohort.
    • Enrollment only: no treatment, discontinuation or in-study rules (crossover, retreatment, rollover), no optional sub-studies, and only the United States version of a country-specific criterion.
    • Gaps: missing references, conflicts and text damaged by conversion get an explicit "Source limitation:" statement; nothing is invented.
    • Protocols: eligibility is searched for throughout the document (study design, part or module sections, appendices, tables, footnotes). The body wins over an abbreviated synopsis, and current text over superseded amendment text.

    How requests run:

    • Validation: each answer is checked by its own contract (parse_screen, parse_cohort_list, parse_criteria): exactly its one field, lowercase "yes" or "no", distinct nonempty names, nonempty criteria that are not a placeholder, and a stop finish. One complete outer Markdown fence is accepted. Duplicate keys, extra fields, surrounding prose and truncation fail validation; an invalid answer is never a rejection.
    • Structured outputs: with a remote backend and structured_outputs: true (the default), each request carries its step's JSON schema as response_format. Local generation relies on validation.
    • Retries: only a failed request is retried, with the full document, the original instructions and the validation error, up to max_attempts (default 3). A cohort whose criteria fail every attempt fails its trial.
    • Concurrency: a remote backend keeps chunk_size * chunks_in_flight requests (500 × 2) in flight, each sent as another returns, and sends later steps first so started trials finish. A local backend gets batches of chunk_size requests of one step, chunks_in_flight at once.
    • Prefix cache: every prompt starts with the same preamble and document, and the criteria prompt puts the cohort list and target last, so a server's prefix cache reuses a trial's document across its requests.
    • Identical criteria: cohorts whose extracted criteria are identical (ignoring whitespace and case) become one row, with the names joined by "; ".

    These settings are under trial.cohorts for listings and protocol_cohort_extraction for protocols, each with an optional prompt_files mapping screen, list and criteria to other packaged prompt files. The section's other keys set the LLM. See configuration.

    Cohort rows¶

    Column Meaning
    cohort_trial_id <trial_id>-cohort-<n>, with n the zero-based cohort number.
    trial_id The source trial.
    cohort_number Zero-based, in document order.
    cohort_name The name from step 2, also for a single population (for example "All participants"). It identifies the cohort and is not part of its criteria or embeddings.
    cohort_eligibility_criteria The cohort's complete criteria from its own request.
    cohort_criteria_source ctgov or protocol (older ctgov outputs say registry).
    cancer_treatment_trial yes on every row.
    multiple_cohorts yes when the trial has more than one cohort.
    trial_input_text The document sent, with debug_mode (listings only).

    Rejected and failed trials get no rows; an empty or all-rejected result keeps the columns.

    Decisions, metadata and QC¶

    A trial's decision (schema trial-cohorts-stepwise-v1) is {"cancer_treatment_trial": "no"} or

    {
  "cancer_treatment_trial": "yes",
  "multiple_cohorts": "yes",
  "cohorts": [
    {"cohort_name": "Part A", "eligibility_criteria": "Inclusion criteria: ..."},
    {"cohort_name": "Part B", "eligibility_criteria": "Inclusion criteria: ..."}
  ]
}
    • attrs: the result's attrs always hold extraction_method (cohorts, or protocol_cohorts from extract_protocol_cohorts), schema_version, representation_version (trial-treatment-cohorts-v5), prompt_sha256, structured_outputs, trial_decisions, trial_rejections (rejected trial ID to decision) and trial_errors (failed trial ID to its last validation error).
    • return_metadata=True: the same keys plus prompt_sha256_by_step, trial_cohort_names, trial_merged_cohorts (cohorts combined for identical criteria), trial_attempts (per step, and per cohort for criteria: finish reason, validation error and serving endpoint), model_metadata and config_snapshot. extract_protocol_cohorts adds source_documents (path, SHA-256, character and page counts, text path) and skipped_completed. debug_mode adds trial_responses, every step's answer and reasoning.
    • return_qc=True (extract_cohorts only): QC rows trials_rejected_cancer_treatment, trials_failed_inference and trials_truncated_llm_response; see QC metrics.
    from matchminer_ai.trials import extract_cohorts

cohorts, metadata, qc = extract_cohorts(listings, config=config, return_metadata=True, return_qc=True)
metadata["trial_rejections"]    # {"NCT07654321": {"cancer_treatment_trial": "no"}}
metadata["trial_errors"]        # trial ID -> last validation error

    Merging equivalent cohorts¶

    merge_equivalent_cohorts takes cohort rows from either extractor (other columns are kept) and sends one request per trial with more than one cohort (trial.cohorts.merge.user.txt), listing the cohorts by number with their criteria.

    • Rule: cohorts belong together when everyone who meets one cohort's criteria would meet the other's. Only the treatment the trial assigns (agent, dose, schedule) may differ. A required background or concurrent therapy is eligibility and keeps cohorts apart. Differences of wording, or a shared criterion one extraction left out, do not. When unsure, the model keeps cohorts apart.
    • Answer: only a grouping, {"groups": [[1, 3], [2]]}, validated as a partition of the cohort numbers and retried on failure.
    • Result: each group becomes its first cohort's row, with the names joined by "; ", cohort_number and cohort_trial_id renumbered, multiple_cohorts recomputed, and merged_from listing the original cohort numbers (JSON). The criteria are the first cohort's; none are regenerated.
    • Failures: a trial whose answer never validates keeps its cohorts unmerged and is listed in merge_errors.
    • Metadata: trial_groups, merge_errors, trial_attempts (with answers and reasoning), skipped_completed, cohorts_in and cohorts_out, with the schema version and prompt hash.

    Key and boilerplate criteria¶

    remove_boilerplate_criteria separates each cohort's key criteria, which define its population, from its boilerplate criteria, the general requirements most cancer trials share, and tags every line as an inclusion or exclusion criterion.

    Boilerplate covers consent, compliance, samples and biopsies, measurable disease, prognosis, performance status, organ function, brain metastases, toxicities of earlier treatment, washout periods of 30 days or less, recovery from recent major surgery, non-cancer conditions, unrelated other cancers, simultaneous participation in another trial, legal and administrative requirements, vaccines, investigator-judgment and study-staff exclusions, and contraception, pregnancy and lactation. Each is boilerplate unless it is central to what the trial studies, such as brain metastasis criteria in a trial for brain metastases.

    • One request per line: each cohort's criteria are split into items, one per nonblank line other than the "Inclusion criteria:" and "Exclusion criteria:" headings. Each item gets its own request (trial.cohorts.deboilerplate.user.txt) showing the cohort's numbered criteria, answered {"criterion_type": "inclusion" | "exclusion", "boilerplate": true | false}.
    • Inclusion or exclusion: an item under a heading takes that heading's type, even when worded negatively. Sub-items, notes and introductory lines take the type of the criterion they belong to.
    • No rewriting: code sorts the unchanged lines, so no criterion is lost or reworded.

    The rows keep cohort_eligibility_criteria unchanged and gain:

    Column Meaning
    cohort_key_criteria The lines that are not boilerplate, under their headings. Read by TrialSpace retrieval and the decision model's match questions.
    cohort_boilerplate_criteria The boilerplate lines. Read by the decision model's boilerplate question.
    cohort_criteria_items Every line with its tags, as a JSON string: a list of {"number", "heading", "text", "criterion_type", "boilerplate"} in the original order. Read by check_eligibility_criteria.
    • Unanswered lines: a line whose answer never validates counts as key, has criterion_type null and is listed in unclassified_items.
    • Unsplittable cohorts: a cohort without lines, or whose lines are all boilerplate, keeps all its criteria as cohort_key_criteria, has cohort_boilerplate_criteria None and is listed in deboilerplate_errors.
    • Metadata: also criterion_type_heading_disagreements (lines whose type differs from their heading), items, items_reused, skipped_completed and trial_cohorts (every line's attempts with answers and reasoning).

    Guideline catalog spaces have no boilerplate criteria. load_guideline_catalog gives each space a cohort_key_criteria: the protocol-style criteria the catalog stores, or for a catalog without them, the space's nine population fields laid out as criteria. See the guideline extraction guide.

    Saved results and reuse¶

    With output_dir, each step writes its per-trial results atomically as each trial finishes, and a rerun with the same directory sends only the requests whose inputs changed.

    File Written by Contents Reused while unchanged
    <trial_id>/extraction.json, <trial_id>/reasoning.json extract_protocol_cohorts The decision, cohort names, combined cohorts, every attempt per step with its serving endpoint, prompt hashes, schema and representation versions and source-document SHA-256; each step's reasoning Prompts, schema and document bytes
    <trial_id>/merge.json merge_equivalent_cohorts The grouping, cohort names and attempts Prompt and the trial's cohort names and criteria
    <trial_id>/deboilerplate.json remove_boilerplate_criteria Per cohort, key_criteria and boilerplate_criteria (or error) and every line's tags and attempts; written once all the trial's lines are answered Per line: prompt, schema and its cohort's name and criteria
    screen.jsonl screen_trials One line per trial: decision and attempts Prompt and the trial's document

    extract_cohorts saves nothing, so process_trials(source="ctgov") extracts listings again on every run and reuses merge and boilerplate results only where the extracted cohorts come out the same.

    matchminer_ai.trials.cohort_run.read_trial_results(trial_dir) reads one trial's files during or after a run and reports its state (complete, stale, not_treatment, extracting or pending) and cohorts; the dashboard uses it.

    Cancer-treatment screen only¶

    screen_trials runs only the screen, with the same prompt, settings (section) and response contract. Rows hold trial_id and either trial_text or a listing's title (or trial_title), brief_summary and eligibility_criteria. It returns trial_id, cancer_treatment_trial ("yes", "no", or None when no answer validated) and screen_error; a trial whose answer never validates is reported, not rejected. Training draws its trial sample from the trials this screen accepts.

    ClinicalTrials.gov records¶

    matchminer_ai.trials.ctgov_records fetches one study from the public ClinicalTrials.gov API v2. Requests carry only the NCT ID, never patient context.

    • fetch_trial_ctgov_document takes an NCT ID or an official study URL and returns the official (or brief) title, brief summary, detailed description and eligibility criteria, with the study URL, retrieval time and last update date. The request always goes to ClinicalTrials.gov with the normalized NCT ID, whatever host a URL names.
    • fetch_trial_eligibility_criteria returns only the eligibility criteria, with line breaks kept, and the same provenance.
    • fetch_trial_study (async) returns the raw study; the GoodOption catalog uses it.
    from matchminer_ai.trials.ctgov_records import fetch_trial_ctgov_document

document = fetch_trial_ctgov_document("NCT01234567")
listings = pd.DataFrame([{"trial_id": document.nct_id, "trial_title": document.trial_title,
                          "brief_summary": document.brief_summary,
                          "eligibility_criteria": document.eligibility_criteria}])

    Synthetic protocols¶

    generate_synthetic_protocols writes one fabricated full protocol per ctgov seed (trial_id and optional title, brief_summary, detailed_description and eligibility_criteria), for training corpora with realistic, often messily specified eligibility text. Each trial gets one of the weighted eligibility layouts in the packaged synthetic_protocol_layouts.json. With output_dir, each trial's protocol.md, reasoning.txt and meta.json are written as it returns and completed trials are skipped on rerun. Outputs are fabricated and must be labeled synthetic wherever they are used. See synthetic protocols.

    :func:`~matchminer_ai.trials.merge_equivalent_cohorts` and

    if not return_metadata:
        return result
    metadata["config_snapshot"] = config_snapshot(resolved)
    return result, metadata

                atomic_json(root / str(trial_id) / "merge.json", {
                    "trial_id": trial_id, **identity(groups_of[trial_id]), "groups": parsed,
                    "cohort_names": list(groups_of[trial_id].cohort_name),
                    "completed_at": datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(timespec="seconds"),
                    "attempts": metadata["trial_attempts"][trial_id]})

        queue.run(record)

        group = groups_of[trial_id].reset_index(drop=True)
        partition = metadata["trial_groups"][trial_id]
        for number, members in enumerate(partition):
            row = group.iloc[members[0]].to_dict()
            row.update(cohort_name="; ".join(str(group.cohort_name.iloc[i]) for i in members),
                       cohort_number=number, cohort_trial_id=f"{trial_id}-cohort-{number}",
                       multiple_cohorts="yes" if len(partition) > 1 else "no",
                       **{MERGED_FROM: json.dumps([int(group.cohort_number.iloc[i]) for i in members])})
            rows.append(row)
    extra = [c for c in frame.columns if c not in COHORT_COLUMNS and c not in ("_order", MERGED_FROM)]
    result = pd.DataFrame(rows, columns=[c for c in COHORT_COLUMNS if c in frame.columns] + extra + [MERGED_FROM])
    if "cohort_number" in result:
        result["cohort_number"] = result.cohort_number.astype("int64")
    metadata["cohorts_in"], metadata["cohorts_out"] = len(frame), len(result)
    return (result, metadata) if return_metadata else result

    cohorts: pd.DataFrame,
    *,
    config: MMAIConfig | None = None,
    section: str = "trial",
    output_dir: str | Path | None = None,
    return_metadata: bool = False,
) -> pd.DataFrame | tuple[pd.DataFrame, dict[str, Any]]:

    ``cohorts`` holds cohort rows (``trial_id``, ``cohort_number``, ``cohort_name``,
    ``cohort_eligibility_criteria``; other columns are kept), extracted or merged. Each row's
    criteria are split into items (:func:`split_criteria`); each item gets one LLM request with

    ctgov listings, "protocol_cohort_extraction" for protocols), whose answer (the item's

    are all boilerplate, keeps its criteria unchanged, with no boilerplate criteria, and is
    reported in ``deboilerplate_errors``. Both are keyed by ``cohort_trial_id`` when the rows have
    it, otherwise ``<trial_id>-cohort-<cohort_number>``.

    Returns the rows in their original order, ``cohort_eligibility_criteria`` unchanged (all the
    criteria), with a ``cohort_key_criteria`` column holding the key criteria (the criteria that are
    not boilerplate; all the criteria for a cohort in ``deboilerplate_errors``) and a
    ``cohort_boilerplate_criteria`` column holding the boilerplate criteria (None for a cohort in
    ``deboilerplate_errors``), and a ``cohort_criteria_items`` column holding every item with its
    tags (:func:`criteria_items`; a JSON string). The metadata's ``criterion_type_heading_disagreements``
    counts items whose criterion type differs from the "Inclusion criteria:" or "Exclusion
    criteria:" label above them. Every item's attempts in
    the metadata's ``trial_cohorts`` (each trial's results as ``deboilerplate.json`` stores them)
    keep the model's answer and reasoning. With ``output_dir``, each trial's results are saved to
    ``<output_dir>/<trial_id>/deboilerplate.json`` as its requests return, and an item's
    classification is reused when the prompt and its cohort's name and criteria are unchanged.
    """
    resolved = config or load_default_preset()
    frame = cohorts.reset_index(drop=True).copy()
    missing = {"trial_id", "cohort_number", "cohort_name", "cohort_eligibility_criteria"} - set(frame)
    if missing:
        raise ValueError(f"cohorts is missing columns: {sorted(missing)}")
    prompt = load_prompt_text(DEBOILERPLATE_PROMPT)
    prompt_sha = hashlib.sha256(prompt.encode()).hexdigest()
    root = Path(output_dir) if output_dir is not None else None
    keys = (list(frame.cohort_trial_id) if "cohort_trial_id" in frame
            else [f"{t}-cohort-{n}" for t, n in zip(frame.trial_id, frame.cohort_number)])
    names = [str(n) for n in frame.cohort_name.fillna("Cohort")]
    texts = [str(t) for t in frame.cohort_eligibility_criteria.fillna("")]
    inputs = [hashlib.sha256(json.dumps([name, text]).encode()).hexdigest() for name, text in zip(names, texts)]
    items = [split_criteria(text) for text in texts]
    metadata = {"schema_version": DEBOILERPLATE_SCHEMA_VERSION, "prompt_sha256": prompt_sha,
                "deboilerplate_errors": {}, "unclassified_items": {}, "skipped_completed": 0,
                "items": sum(map(len, items)), "items_reused": 0}
    labels: dict[tuple[int, int], dict[str, Any]] = {}  # (row, item number) -> its two tags
    attempts: dict[tuple[int, int], list[dict[str, Any]]] = {}
    failed: dict[tuple[int, int], str] = {}
    rows_of: dict[Any, list[int]] = {}
    for row, trial_id in enumerate(frame.trial_id):
        rows_of.setdefault(trial_id, []).append(row)

    pending = []
    for trial_id, rows in rows_of.items():
        path = root / str(trial_id) / "deboilerplate.json" if root else None
        record = json.loads(path.read_text(encoding="utf-8")) if path is not None and path.exists() else {}
        current = (record.get("schema_version") == DEBOILERPLATE_SCHEMA_VERSION
                   and record.get("prompt_sha256") == prompt_sha)
        saved = {e["input_sha256"]: e for e in record.get("cohorts", []) if current}
        for row in rows:

                    if "boilerplate" in i and "criterion_type" in i}
            for number in range(1, len(items[row]) + 1):

                    labels[row, number] = {k: done[number][k] for k in ("criterion_type", "boilerplate")}
                    attempts[row, number] = done[number].get("attempts", [])
                    metadata["items_reused"] += 1
                else:  # a cohort's items stay together so their shared prefix is cached
                    messages = build_deboilerplate_messages(names[row], items[row], number, prompt)
                    pending.append((row, number, 1, messages, messages))
            if items[row] and all((row, n) in labels for n in range(1, len(items[row]) + 1)):
                metadata["skipped_completed"] += 1

    def result(row):
        """(criteria, boilerplate or None, error or None) once every item has an answer, else None."""
        numbers = range(1, len(items[row]) + 1)
        if not all((row, n) in labels or (row, n) in failed for n in numbers):
            return None
        criteria, boilerplate = gather_criteria(items[row], [labels.get((row, n), {}).get("boilerplate", False)
                                                             for n in numbers])
        if not criteria:
            return texts[row], None, ("no criteria to classify" if not items[row]

        entries = []
        for r in rows_of[trial_id]:
            numbered = [{"number": n, "label": label, "text": line, **labels.get((r, n), {}),
                         **({"error": failed[r, n]} if (r, n) in failed else {}),

            entries.append({**entry, "items": numbered})
        return entries

            "trial_id": trial_id, "schema_version": DEBOILERPLATE_SCHEMA_VERSION, "prompt_sha256": prompt_sha,
            "cohorts": entries_of(trial_id), "completed_at": datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(timespec="seconds")})

    if pending:
        runtime, settings = _section(resolved, section, "trial_cohorts_deboilerplate", DEBOILERPLATE_JSON_SCHEMA)
        metadata["structured_outputs"] = settings["structured"]
        queue = RequestQueue(get_llm_backend(resolved), {None: runtime}, chunk_size=settings["chunk_size"],
                             chunks_in_flight=settings["chunks_in_flight"],
                             model_metadata_cache_dir=resolved.model_metadata_cache_dir)
        unanswered: dict[Any, int] = {}  # each trial's items still waiting for a final answer
        for row, number, attempt, messages, original in pending:
            queue.add((row, number, attempt, original), messages)
            unanswered[frame.trial_id.iloc[row]] = unanswered.get(frame.trial_id.iloc[row], 0) + 1

        def record(item, answer, reasoning, finish, provenance):
            row, number, attempt, original = item
            entry = {"attempt": attempt, "finish_reason": finish, "answer": answer, "reasoning": reasoning}
            if provenance is not None:
                entry["response_metadata"] = provenance
            attempts.setdefault((row, number), []).append(entry)
            try:
                labels[row, number] = parse_deboilerplated(answer, finish)
            except ValueError as exc:
                entry["validation_error"] = str(exc)
                if attempt < settings["max_attempts"]:
                    queue.add((row, number, attempt + 1, original), original + [{"role": "user", "content": (
                        f"The preceding attempt failed validation: {exc}. Return a complete replacement "
                        "JSON object following the original instructions.")}])
                    return
                failed[row, number] = str(exc)
            trial_id = frame.trial_id.iloc[row]
            unanswered[trial_id] -= 1
            if root is not None and not unanswered[trial_id]:  # a trial is saved when its last item is done
                save(trial_id)

        queue.run(record)

    outcomes = [result(r) for r in range(len(frame))]
    for r, (_, _, error) in enumerate(outcomes):
        if error is not None:
            metadata["deboilerplate_errors"][keys[r]] = error
        unclassified = [n for n in range(1, len(items[r]) + 1) if (r, n) in failed]
        if unclassified:
            metadata["unclassified_items"][keys[r]] = {n: failed[r, n] for n in unclassified}
    result_frame = frame.copy()
    result_frame[KEY_COLUMN] = [criteria for criteria, _, _ in outcomes]
    result_frame[BOILERPLATE_COLUMN] = pd.Series([boilerplate for _, boilerplate, _ in outcomes], dtype=object)
    result_frame[ITEMS_COLUMN] = [criteria_items(items[r], [labels.get((r, n)) for n in range(1, len(items[r]) + 1)])
                                  for r in range(len(frame))]
    metadata["criterion_type_heading_disagreements"] = sum(
        1 for (r, n), tag in labels.items()
        if heading_type(items[r][n - 1][0]) not in (None, tag["criterion_type"]))
    metadata["cohorts"] = len(frame)
    metadata["trial_cohorts"] = {trial_id: entries_of(trial_id) for trial_id in rows_of}
    if root is not None:
        for trial_id in rows_of:  # cohorts whose items were all reused, or that have no items
            if not (root / str(trial_id) / "deboilerplate.json").exists():
                save(trial_id)
    return (result_frame, metadata) if return_metadata else result_frame

    def screen_trials(
    trials: pd.DataFrame,
    *,
    config: MMAIConfig | None = None,
    section: str = "trial",
    output_dir: str | Path | None = None,
    return_metadata: bool = False,
) -> pd.DataFrame | tuple[pd.DataFrame, dict[str, Any]]:
    """Decide for each trial whether it is a cancer treatment trial.

    ``trials`` holds ``trial_id`` and either ``trial_text`` or a listing's ``title`` (or
    ``trial_title``), ``brief_summary`` and ``eligibility_criteria``. Each trial gets the
    workflow's screen request with the LLM settings of ``section`` ("trial" or
    "protocol_cohort_extraction"); an answer that fails validation is retried up to that
    section's ``max_attempts``, and a trial whose answer never validates is not rejected but
    reported with its error. With ``output_dir``, each trial's attempts (answers, reasoning and
    response metadata) are appended to ``<output_dir>/screen.jsonl`` as its requests return, and a
    saved decision is reused when the prompt and the trial's document are unchanged.

    Returns one row per trial, in input order: ``trial_id``, ``cancer_treatment_trial`` ("yes",
    "no", or None when no answer validated) and ``screen_error``. The metadata holds the schema
    version, prompt hash, counts, errors and every trial's attempts.
    """
    resolved = config or load_default_preset()
    frame = trials.reset_index(drop=True)
    if "trial_id" not in frame or frame.trial_id.isna().any() or frame.trial_id.duplicated().any():
        raise ValueError("trials needs unique, present trial_id values")
    ids = [str(t) for t in frame.trial_id]
    texts = _documents(frame)
    prompts = load_prompts()
    prompt_sha = hashlib.sha256(prompts["screen"].encode()).hexdigest()
    inputs = [hashlib.sha256(text.encode()).hexdigest() for text in texts]
    path = Path(output_dir) / SCREEN_FILE if output_dir is not None else None
    saved: dict[tuple[str, str], dict[str, Any]] = {}
    if path is not None and path.exists():
        for line in path.read_text().splitlines():
            entry = json.loads(line)
            if entry.get("prompt_sha256") == prompt_sha and entry.get("decision") in ("yes", "no"):
                saved[entry["trial_id"], entry["input_sha256"]] = entry
    decisions: dict[int, str] = {}
    errors: dict[int, str] = {}
    attempts: dict[int, list[dict[str, Any]]] = {}
    metadata: dict[str, Any] = {"schema_version": SCREEN_SCHEMA_VERSION, "prompt_sha256": prompt_sha,
                                "trials": len(frame), "reused": 0, "structured_outputs": None}
    pending = []
    for row, (trial_id, digest) in enumerate(zip(ids, inputs)):
        if (trial_id, digest) in saved:
            decisions[row], attempts[row] = saved[trial_id, digest]["decision"], saved[trial_id, digest]["attempts"]
            metadata["reused"] += 1
        else:
            messages = build_messages("screen", texts[row], prompts)
            pending.append((row, 1, messages, messages))

    def write(rows):
        if path is None or not rows:
            return
        path.parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        now = datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(timespec="seconds")
        with path.open("a") as handle:
            for row in rows:
                entry = {"trial_id": ids[row], "schema_version": SCREEN_SCHEMA_VERSION, "prompt_sha256": prompt_sha,
                         "input_sha256": inputs[row], "decision": decisions.get(row),
                         **({"error": errors[row]} if row in errors else {}),
                         "attempts": attempts.get(row, []), "completed_at": now}
                handle.write(json.dumps(entry) + "\n")

    if pending:
        runtime, settings = _section(resolved, section, "trial_cohorts_screen", JSON_SCHEMAS["screen"])
        metadata["structured_outputs"] = settings["structured"]
        queue = RequestQueue(get_llm_backend(resolved), {None: runtime}, chunk_size=settings["chunk_size"],
                             chunks_in_flight=settings["chunks_in_flight"],
                             model_metadata_cache_dir=resolved.model_metadata_cache_dir)
        for row, attempt, messages, original in pending:
            queue.add((row, attempt, original), messages)
        finished: list[int] = []

        def record(item, answer, reasoning, finish, provenance):
            row, attempt, original = item
            entry = {"attempt": attempt, "finish_reason": finish, "answer": answer, "reasoning": reasoning}
            if provenance is not None:
                entry["response_metadata"] = provenance
            attempts.setdefault(row, []).append(entry)
            try:
                decisions[row] = parse_screen(answer, finish)
            except ValueError as exc:
                entry["validation_error"] = str(exc)
                if attempt < settings["max_attempts"]:
                    queue.add((row, attempt + 1, original), original + [{"role": "user", "content": (
                        f"The preceding attempt failed validation: {exc}. Return a complete replacement "
                        "JSON object following the original instructions and the full document.")}])
                    return
                errors[row] = str(exc)
            finished.append(row)

        def flush():  # finished trials are appended to the file as the requests go
            write(finished)
            finished.clear()

        queue.run(record, progress=flush)

    result = pd.DataFrame({"trial_id": ids,
                           "cancer_treatment_trial": pd.Series([decisions.get(r) for r in range(len(ids))], dtype=object),
                           "screen_error": pd.Series([errors.get(r) for r in range(len(ids))], dtype=object)})
    metadata.update(accepted=sum(d == "yes" for d in decisions.values()),
                    rejected=sum(d == "no" for d in decisions.values()),
                    screen_errors={ids[r]: e for r, e in errors.items()},
                    trial_attempts={ids[r]: attempts.get(r, []) for r in range(len(ids))})
    return (result, metadata) if return_metadata else result

    def generate_synthetic_protocols(
    seeds: pd.DataFrame,
    *,
    config: MMAIConfig | None = None,
    output_dir: str | Path | None = None,
    return_metadata: bool = False,
    progress_callback: SyntheticProtocolProgress | None = None,
) -> pd.DataFrame | tuple[pd.DataFrame, dict[str, Any]]:
    """Write one synthetic full protocol per ctgov seed.

    ``seeds`` has one row per trial with a unique ``trial_id`` and optional
    ``title``, ``brief_summary``, ``detailed_description`` and
    ``eligibility_criteria`` strings (missing fields are marked as not provided).
    The record is a seed, not a specification: the protocol may differ from it,
    and its eligibility section follows one of the weighted layouts in
    ``synthetic_protocol_layouts.json``, chosen by a stable hash of the trial ID.

    Requests go through a :class:`~matchminer_ai.llm.request_queue.RequestQueue`:
    remote backends keep ``synthetic_protocol_generation.chunk_size *
    chunks_in_flight`` requests in flight, each sent as another returns, so one
    slow protocol holds up only itself; other backends get chunks of
    ``chunk_size``, up to ``chunks_in_flight`` at once. Remote runs use the shared
    endpoint pool, so adaptive concurrency and endpoint registries
    (``remote.server_urls_file``) apply as for other workloads. With
    ``output_dir``, each trial's ``protocol.md``, ``reasoning.txt`` and
    ``meta.json`` are written when its request returns, trials already completed
    with the same prompt are skipped, and the result holds file paths instead of
    text. Empty or truncated outputs are retried up to ``max_attempts``.

    Outputs are fabricated training documents, not real protocols, and must not
    be presented as trial criteria. ctgov text reaches the configured LLM.
    """
    resolved = config or load_default_preset()
    if not isinstance(resolved, MMAIConfig):
        raise TypeError("config must be an MMAIConfig instance or None.")
    settings = dict(resolved.synthetic_protocol_generation)
    if not settings:
        raise ValueError("Config is missing synthetic_protocol_generation settings.")
    if not isinstance(seeds, pd.DataFrame) or "trial_id" not in seeds:
        raise ValueError("seeds must be a DataFrame with a trial_id column.")
    ids = seeds["trial_id"]
    if ids.isna().any() or ids.astype(str).str.strip().eq("").any() or not ids.astype(str).is_unique:
        raise ValueError("trial_id must be nonempty and unique")
    chunk_size = settings.get("chunk_size", 500)
    chunks_in_flight = settings.get("chunks_in_flight", 2)
    max_attempts = settings.get("max_attempts", 3)
    for name, value, upper in (("chunk_size", chunk_size, None), ("chunks_in_flight", chunks_in_flight, 16),
                               ("max_attempts", max_attempts, 10)):
        if type(value) is not int or value < 1 or (upper and value > upper):
            raise ValueError(f"synthetic_protocol_generation.{name} must be an integer from 1"
                             + (f" to {upper}" if upper else ""))

    system, user = load_prompt_text(SYNTHETIC_PROTOCOL_SYSTEM_PROMPT), load_prompt_text(SYNTHETIC_PROTOCOL_USER_PROMPT)
    layouts = load_synthetic_protocol_layouts(settings.get("layouts_file", SYNTHETIC_PROTOCOL_LAYOUTS))
    prompt_sha256 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps([system, user, layouts], sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    llm_only = {key: deepcopy(value) for key, value in settings.items()
                if key in {"local", "remote", "reasoning_parser", "sampling_profile", "reasoning_effort"}}
    runtime = build_llm_runtime_config("synthetic_protocol_generation", llm_only, config=resolved)
    root = Path(output_dir) if output_dir is not None else None

    records = seeds.assign(trial_id=ids.astype(str)).to_dict("records")
    rows, pending, metadata = {}, [], {
        "prompt_sha256": prompt_sha256, "trial_errors": {}, "trial_attempts": {}, "model_metadata": {},
        "skipped_completed": 0,
    }
    for record in records:
        trial_id = record["trial_id"]
        layout_index = choose_layout(trial_id, layouts)
        done = _completed(root / trial_id, prompt_sha256) if root else None
        if done:
            metadata["skipped_completed"] += 1
            rows[trial_id] = dict(trial_id=trial_id, layout_index=layout_index, finish_reason="stop",
                                  attempts=done.get("attempt"), protocol=None,
                                  protocol_path=str(root / trial_id / "protocol.md"))
            continue
        pending.append((record, layout_index, 1))
    total = len(records)
    _emit(progress_callback, "generate", len(rows), total, f"{len(pending)} protocol(s) to generate")

    def messages(record, layout_index):
        return build_synthetic_protocol_messages(record, layouts[layout_index]["text"], system=system, user=user)

    def record_answer(item, text, reasoning, finish, provenance):
        """Save an accepted output, or queue a retry."""
        record, layout_index, attempt = item
        trial_id, text = record["trial_id"], str(text or "")
        attempt_record = {"attempt": attempt, "finish_reason": finish, "characters": len(text)}
        if provenance is not None:
            attempt_record["response_metadata"] = provenance
        metadata["trial_attempts"].setdefault(trial_id, []).append(attempt_record)
        if finish != "stop" or not text.strip():
            metadata["trial_errors"][trial_id] = f"finish_reason={finish}, {len(text)} characters"
            if attempt < max_attempts:
                queue.add((record, layout_index, attempt + 1), messages(record, layout_index))
            return
        metadata["trial_errors"].pop(trial_id, None)
        row = dict(trial_id=trial_id, layout_index=layout_index, finish_reason=finish, attempts=attempt,
                   protocol=text, protocol_path=None)
        if root:
            run_dir = root / trial_id
            run_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
            _write_atomic(run_dir / "protocol.md", text)
            _write_atomic(run_dir / "reasoning.txt", str(reasoning or ""))
            seed = {key: record.get(key) for key in ("trial_id", *SEED_FIELDS) if key in record}
            _write_atomic(run_dir / "meta.json", json.dumps(dict(
                trial_id=trial_id, prompt_sha256=prompt_sha256, layout_index=layout_index, attempt=attempt,
                finish_reason=finish, completed_at=datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(timespec="seconds"),
                response_metadata=attempt_record.get("response_metadata"), seed=seed,
            ), indent=2, default=str))
            row.update(protocol=None, protocol_path=str(run_dir / "protocol.md"))
        rows[trial_id] = row

    if pending:
        queue = RequestQueue(get_llm_backend(resolved), {None: runtime}, chunk_size=chunk_size,
                             chunks_in_flight=chunks_in_flight,
                             model_metadata_cache_dir=resolved.model_metadata_cache_dir)
        for record, layout_index, attempt in pending:
            queue.add((record, layout_index, attempt), messages(record, layout_index))
        # Results, files and retry bookkeeping are handled only in this thread.
        metadata["model_metadata"] = queue.run(record_answer, progress=lambda: _emit(
            progress_callback, "generate", len(rows), total,
            f"{len(rows)} of {total} generated; {len(metadata['trial_errors'])} awaiting retry or failed"))

    columns = ["trial_id", "layout_index", "finish_reason", "attempts", "protocol", "protocol_path"]
    result = pd.DataFrame([rows[r["trial_id"]] for r in records if r["trial_id"] in rows], columns=columns)
    result.attrs.update(prompt_sha256=prompt_sha256, trial_errors=dict(metadata["trial_errors"]))
    _emit(progress_callback, "complete", len(rows), total, "Synthetic protocol generation finished")
    if not return_metadata:
        return result
    metadata["config_snapshot"] = config_snapshot(resolved)
    return result, metadata

    Returns the same inventory DataFrame as ``list_guidelines``. Source text is
    subject to NCI's page-specific reuse exceptions; no images are downloaded.

    from ._guideline_sources import load_guideline
    from .guidelines import list_guidelines

    if (
        isinstance(request_timeout, bool)
        or not isinstance(request_timeout, (int, float))
        or request_timeout <= 0
    ):
        raise ValueError("request_timeout must be positive")
    if progress_callback is not None and not callable(progress_callback):
        raise TypeError("progress_callback must be callable or None")
    output = Path(output_dir).resolve()
    package = Path(__file__).resolve().parents[1]
    checkout = package.parent.parent
    for protected in [
        package,
        *([checkout] if (checkout / "pyproject.toml").exists() else []),

                progress_callback(message)

                logging.getLogger(__name__).exception("PDQ progress callback failed")

        identity = {"source_format": FORMAT, "summaries": requested}
        if plan.exists() and read_json(plan) != identity:
            raise ValueError("PDQ source selection changed; use a new output directory")
        if not plan.exists() and any(p.name != ".run.lock" for p in output.iterdir()):

                "PDQ snapshot output must be empty or an identical resumable library"
            )

                manifest = folder / "manifest.json"
                if manifest.exists():

                        saved.metadata.get("requested_urls") != urls

                            "Existing PDQ snapshot has a different source selection"

                        )

                    for number, section in enumerate(sections, 1):
                        path = f"sections/s{number:04d}.md"
                        text = section["text"] + "\n"
                        atomic_text(folder / path, text)
                        generated[path] = digest(text.encode("utf-8"))
                        records.append(
                            {k: v for k, v in section.items() if k != "text"}
                            | {"path": path, "section_number": number}
                        )
                    source_hash = digest([d["sha256"] for d in documents])
                    metadata = {
                        "generator": GENERATOR,
                        "source_format": FORMAT,
                        "source_kind": "nci_pdq",
                        "is_clinical_practice_guideline": False,
                        "title": " / ".join(d["title"] for d in documents),
                        "version": "snapshot-" + source_hash[:16],

                        "requested_urls": urls,
                        "documents": documents,
                        "page_count": len(records),
                        "pages": records,
                        "generated_files": generated,
                        "warnings": list(
                            dict.fromkeys(w for s in sections for w in s["warnings"])
                        ),
                        "notice": SOURCE_NOTICE,
                    }
                    atomic_json(manifest, metadata)
                    load_guideline(library, disease)
                    notify(f"{disease}: verified {len(records)} PDQ source sections")
                entries.append(
                    {
                        "folder": disease,
                        "title": metadata["title"],
                        "version": metadata["version"],
                        "pages": metadata["page_count"],
                        "source_sha256": metadata["source_sha256"],
                    }
                )

            library / "manifest.json",
            {"generator": GENERATOR, "source_format": FORMAT, "guidelines": entries},
        )
