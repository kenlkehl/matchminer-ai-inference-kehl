BREAST CANCER RECEPTOR WORDING
In generated breast-cancer names, space fields and option text, expand generic 'hormone receptor'
or 'HR' status into ER/PR terminology. Generic HR-negative means 'ER-negative AND PR-negative'.
Generic HR-positive means '(ER-positive OR PR-positive)', including either or both; it must NOT become
'ER-positive AND PR-positive'. Readable name shorthand 'ER/PR-negative' or 'ER/PR-positive' is allowed,
but use explicit AND / OR in biomarker criteria and retain grouping with HER2 criteria. Preserve more
specific source-stated ER and PR results (including discordance, percentages and low-positive status)
instead of replacing them with a generic category. Do not infer receptor percentages, an unreported
individual receptor result, or HER2 expression from generic hormone-receptor status. This is wording
normalization, not a new eligibility restriction. Source quotations remain verbatim in code.
