TRIALSPACE FIELD MEANINGS AND BOOLEAN LOGIC
A clinical space is a unique combination of patient age, sex, primary cancer type,
histology, disease burden, prior treatments required/excluded, and biomarkers required/excluded,
as in extraction of clinical trial eligibility. Here the supplied source defines the population
for a care decision, not eligibility for a trial. Extract patient criteria, not a section summary,
regimen description, study arm, or list of reasons a therapy might be chosen.
CRITICAL: Each space must contain ALL information needed to define that population on its own.
Only the nine fields become TrialSpace embedding text. A criterion in the name, source quotation,
option menu or uncertainty does not substitute for its appropriate field. Never use "same as above".
NA means no source-supported restriction for that field, not "see the name or another field".

Use the nine fields as typed clinical concepts, following the MatchMiner-AI trial primer.
For EVERY clause, ask what fact about the patient it asserts and put it only in the matching field:
- age_range_allowed: age criteria only; do not infer an adult cutoff from silence.
- sex_allowed: sex criteria only; breast cancer does not imply a female-only population.
- cancer_type_allowed: spelled-out primary cancer type/site.
- histology_allowed: histologic/morphologic subtype. Receptor status belongs in biomarkers.
- cancer_burden_allowed: extent, stage, disease sites, resectability, residual disease,
  disease-specific prognostic risk and disease-severity states such as visceral crisis.
  This is NOT a catch-all for the entire population definition. "Untreated", "first-line",
  prior surgery, previous systemic therapy and response/resistance are not disease burden.
  Visceral crisis is a current disease state, NOT a prior treatment or treatment response.
  Do not infer visceral crisis merely from visceral metastases. Exclude performance status,
  measurable-disease requirements, life expectancy and general organ-function/fitness criteria;
  option-specific suitability stays with the option.
- prior_treatment_required: treatment/exposure that must have occurred and the source-defined
  response/resistance to that treatment/class. Endocrine refractoriness belongs here as a
  treatment-response condition, not in burden or biomarkers. Preserve the source wording without
  inventing named failed drugs, prior-regimen counts, or timing. De novo/intrinsic resistance must
  not be expanded into an invented history of treatment. Do not treat every reason to choose a
  therapy as prior treatment: visceral crisis, symptomatic disease and unresectability are not
  treatment histories. Planned/recommended treatment belongs in the menu, not either prior field.
  Ask "What treatment has this patient already received, and what response is required?"
  Treatment ineligibility, intolerance anticipated before exposure, contraindications and poor
  performance status do not establish a history of receiving or failing that treatment.
  Describe the actual prior drug/class, disease setting and source-required response; "second-line"
  or "one prior line" is not an adequate history. Counts require a source-defined counting unit.
- prior_treatment_excluded: the treatment/exposure that must NOT have occurred, with its
  source-defined setting/sequence. Excluding prior cytotoxic therapy does not exclude endocrine
  therapy. Response-based exclusions must identify the source-supported treatment/class.
  Ask "What prior treatment would disqualify this patient from this source-defined population?"
  Express "untreated" or "treatment-naive" here using the treatment and setting actually stated
  by the source. For example, "no previous systemic treatment for advanced disease" excludes
  systemic treatment FOR THAT SETTING, not all surgery, radiotherapy or adjuvant therapy ever.
  Do not label a space first-line/untreated while leaving its only history restriction in the name.
  "Ineligible for drug A" is NOT equivalent to "must not have received drug A". Keep suitability
  in option conditions; when the source does not resolve a history restriction, report that gap
  without inventing a line label, exposure, drug class, count or treatment setting.
- biomarkers_required / biomarkers_excluded: required/disqualifying molecular, germline or
  protein/receptor findings, including source-supported assay, score and threshold. A drug's
  target is not automatically a required biomarker. Treatment resistance is not receptor status.
  Encode EVERY source-defined molecular or receptor restriction explicitly in biomarkers_required
  or biomarkers_excluded, regardless of whether it also appears in the population's name.
  Required negative results belong in biomarkers_required; biomarkers_excluded describes findings
  that disqualify a patient, not a list of negative test results. Do not encode molecular or receptor
  restrictions in histology_allowed. Preserve any separately stated histologic/morphologic restriction;
  use NA for histology_allowed when the source states none. The population name is display metadata,
  not part of the TrialSpace embedding text, and cannot substitute for any of the nine fields.

General treatment suitability (for example, ineligibility for immunotherapy or targeted therapy)
belongs in the applicable option's conditions, not in prior treatment or biomarkers. Do not create
an otherwise identical space solely to encode such suitability. Histology is morphology, not a
receptor shorthand; disease burden is extent/risk, not all the criteria written under a heading.

Requirements in DIFFERENT fields apply together (AND). Do not move the two sides of an OR into
separate fields of one space: that changes the represented population. Within a field, explicitly
parenthesize OR alternatives when they share an AND condition: write '(A OR B) AND C', not
'A OR B AND C', when C is required for both A and B. During extraction or
consolidation, split source-supported alternatives spanning different fields into separate,
self-contained spaces, even if they share a treatment menu. For example, a branch defined by
'visceral crisis OR endocrine refractory' needs a visceral-crisis space with that criterion in
cancer_burden_allowed and a separate endocrine-refractory space with that criterion in
prior_treatment_required. Copy all shared source-supported restrictions into both. The
visceral-crisis alternative must not acquire a requirement for endocrine refractoriness, and the
endocrine-refractory alternative must not acquire a requirement for visceral crisis. These spaces
may overlap; do not invent absence of the other condition to make them mutually exclusive.
Splitting a mixed-field alternative does not authorize expanding the task's clinical scope.
For example, a fictional source population "metastatic cancer with age <40 OR marker X-positive"
requires two spaces: metastatic cancer with age_range_allowed <40, and metastatic cancer with
biomarkers_required marker X-positive. Repeat the shared disease criteria in both. Do not put
age and marker status into cancer_burden_allowed, require both in one space, or invent marker
negativity/age >=40 to make the alternatives disjoint. Preserve the source's OR; never silently
replace it with AND to make a definition narrower. Separately supported cancer types, histologies
or treatment-defining biomarker alternatives also need self-contained spaces, not a mixed list.
During consolidation, preserve the input population's shared prior-treatment and biomarker
restrictions in EVERY resulting alternative. For a first-line input excluding a biomarker, do not
add later-line states or states requiring that biomarker from a neighboring table row. If a source
contradicts an input restriction, report that conflict rather than silently changing the scope.
Do not discard either alternative or weaken both into an unrestricted state. If the source cannot
resolve a boundary, state the uncertainty. Selection copies existing definitions; detail generation
and citation-only repair keep fixed fields unchanged and must report gaps instead of repairing them.

CRITICAL FINAL CHECK WHEN CREATING OR CONSOLIDATING DEFINITIONS:
1. Read the nine fields without the name or options. Do they define the full source-supported
   population, including negative biomarkers and the stated treatment setting/history?
2. Check EVERY clause against its field's meaning. Move misplaced criteria to their proper fields;
   split mixed-field OR alternatives when necessary, repeating shared criteria in every space.
3. Compare the resulting AND/OR population with the source. Do not broaden, narrow, omit an
   alternative or invent a restriction just to fit the schema. Keep suitability with options.
4. Correct an identified field-placement or logic error now. Writing the error in uncertainties
   does NOT make a known-wrong definition acceptable. Uncertainties are for unresolved source
   evidence, not permission to keep a correctable representation error.
These corrections belong in extraction/consolidation. Tasks that hold an existing definition
fixed (selection, detail generation, citation-only repair) must still obey their no-rewrite
contract and report conflicts. A valid JSON shape or a plausible state name is insufficient.
