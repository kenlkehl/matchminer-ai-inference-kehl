POPULATION SPECIFICITY: ONE CURRENT TREATMENT DECISION
A space describes the population at ONE current treatment decision, not every treatment the patient
might receive over the rest of the illness. A metastatic/recurrent subtype alone is insufficient
when the supplied guideline divides its menu by treatment line, prior exposure, resistance or response.

Read treatment tables, their row/column headings, branch labels and footnotes before defining the
space. First-line, second-line and third-line-and-beyond branches with different menus are DIFFERENT
spaces, even when cancer type, stage and receptor status are identical. Put the current treatment
setting/line in the name and encode its source-supported treatment-history boundaries in
prior_treatment_required / prior_treatment_excluded. cancer_burden_allowed describes disease extent,
stage, resectability, residual disease and disease-specific prognostic risk; it must NOT contain
treatment-line labels, counts of prior lines or prior-treatment criteria. This follows the
MatchMiner-AI inference TrialSpace field meanings. Do not hide a population's
defining treatment line only in option conditions or its name while leaving its nine-field definition
broad. A first-line branch excludes prior treatment in the SOURCE-DEFINED TREATMENT SEQUENCE
FOR THAT ADVANCED SETTING, not all prior cancer therapy. Identify whether the source counts all
systemic therapy, endocrine-based therapy, chemotherapy or another specific sequence. First-line
chemotherapy after endocrine therapy must NOT exclude that prior endocrine therapy. Prior
neoadjuvant/adjuvant treatment must not be excluded merely because advanced treatment is first-line.
A later-line label alone
does not establish a specific failed drug, intolerance, refractory disease or progression mechanism.
Keep prior drug/class exposure, response, resistance and timing constraints when the source specifies
them; do not invent them. Do not count treatment in a different setting toward a line requirement.

OUTPUT CONTRACT: express treatment-line boundaries as prior-treatment criteria, including BOTH
lower and upper bounds when the source defines a bounded line or shared bin. For the SOURCE-DEFINED
sequence and setting: second-line means exactly one prior line; second-line OR third-line means
one OR two prior lines (not merely 'at least one'); third-line and beyond means at least two prior
lines. First-line requires no prior line in that sequence/setting: put the treatment that must NOT
have been received in prior_treatment_excluded, leaving prior_treatment_required as NA unless other
prior therapy is actually required. The excluded field lists the disallowed treatment itself, NOT
'No prior treatment', which would reverse the exclusion's meaning. Keep ordinal labels out of
cancer_burden_allowed. Do not infer which drugs comprised prior lines from the ordinal label alone.

Follow the source's actual decision branches. Split source-defined predictive biomarker branches
(including test thresholds or prior targeted-drug exposure) when they change the available menu.
Do not create every possible biomarker cross-product or one state per drug. Preserve a source-defined
shared bin such as 'third line and beyond' when it has one menu; do not fabricate separate third,
fourth and fifth lines. Option-only suitability, organ-function, availability and supportive-care
conditions remain with the option unless the source defines a distinct disease-state branch.

During consolidation, retain each explicit treatment-line/prior-exposure population. Broad routing
pages and subtype summaries are umbrellas, not alternatives to their specific treatment branches.
Use source pages to split a broad candidate whose option conditions reveal different treatment
decision populations. Do not merge first-line and later-line branches back together, even if they
share some drugs. Semantic duplicates phrased as 'Breast Cancer; Invasive' versus 'Invasive Breast
Cancer' do not justify parallel umbrella states. Before finishing, compare source treatment-line
branches with the actual nine-field definitions: a meaningful distinction only in the name is lost.

In a detail record, include the COMPLETE menu for the CURRENT defined population only. Treatments
after a future progression, after another treatment line, or in a different resectability/recurrence
state belong to a DIFFERENT space, not this menu with a hypothetical condition. Preserve alternatives
within the present state. An option condition cannot override or contradict the canonical definition.
If the fixed definition is still too broad to locate one source-defined treatment decision, explicitly
report that granularity gap in uncertainties; do not pretend the all-lines menu is a specific state.

These specificity rules REFINE THE ACTIVE TASK'S SCOPE; they never widen it. Extraction is anchored
to PRIMARY pages. Consolidation refines only the supplied clinical populations, not every population
mentioned in background pages. Detail generation remains within its one fixed canonical definition.
