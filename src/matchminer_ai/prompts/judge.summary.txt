WHAT TO JUDGE
statements: one entry for every numbered statement
- verdict:
  "supported": the notes support it. A date may be as approximate as the notes are.
  "unsupported": the notes do not support it: nothing in the notes says it, or it is more specific or more certain than the notes.
  "contradicted": the notes say otherwise: a wrong value, date, drug, dose, site, laterality, stage, biomarker result, response or status.
  "outdated": true earlier, but later notes changed it, and the instructions ask for the current state (for example current extent, ongoing treatment, the most recent performance status or laboratory values).
  "no_claim": a heading or label with no content to check.
- severity: "none" for supported and no_claim. Otherwise "major" when the error would change a trial-eligibility or treatment judgment: cancer type, histology, stage or extent, metastatic sites including the central nervous system, biomarkers, the agents, dates and order of lines of therapy, current treatment, response or progression, performance status, organ function where it matters, clinically important other conditions, earlier trial participation, hospice or a decision to stop treatment. "minor" for anything else, such as a date off by a month or a value of no consequence.
- evidence: for every statement that is not supported, one to three items, each the date of the note (as in its header) and a short quote copied exactly from that note that shows the problem; quotes are checked against the notes. For a supported statement, evidence may be empty.
- note: for a problem, say what is wrong and what the notes say instead; empty otherwise.

omissions: information the instructions ask for, that the notes document, and that the summary leaves out or states so incompletely that it misleads
- Count only what the instructions ask for, and only as much as they ask: for example the most recent value of each routine laboratory test, not earlier values, and the most recent tumor marker trend, not every marker.
- Give the section it belongs to, a severity (major or minor, by the same rule), a description, and evidence (date and exact quote, as above).
- Use the section "other" only for something the instructions ask for that fits no other section.

overall

- summary: two to four sentences on what is wrong, or that the summary is accurate and complete.
