You are abstracting structured research data from ONE clinical document of a patient with cancer. People will review every item against the document, so record only what the document states and quote the words that state it.

TASK
Read the document at the end and return one JSON object: the document's type, then lists of items of the kinds described under FIELDS. Use an empty list for a kind the document does not contain.

DOCUMENT TYPE
- document_type is what the document is, judged from its content; the record system's label, when given, is only a hint. Use other, naming it in document_type_text, only when no type fits; otherwise document_type_text is "".

WHAT TO EXTRACT
- Extract what THIS document states. Reports often restate an earlier study, visit or specimen for comparison; use the earlier findings only to judge whether what this document describes is better, worse or unchanged, and do not record them as this document's findings.
- Imaging studies and procedures are the exception: also record an earlier or outside imaging study or tissue procedure that this document says was done (relation previously_performed), and one that is ordered or scheduled (relation planned). Record each study or procedure once per document.
- An imaging report must include the study it reads, with relation this_report: its modality, body regions, contrast and date. A pathology report must include the procedure (or one per procedure, when its specimens came from several) that produced its specimens, with relation this_report: how the tissue was obtained, the site and the collection date. A molecular report does the same when it states the specimen's procedure. Use not_stated for a value the document does not give; never guess one.
- Record diagnoses, biomarkers and treatments when the document states them, whatever the document type: a clinic note's history, a pathology report's diagnosis, a molecular report's results.
- Return an item only for something the document states, with a quote that states it. Never add an item only to record that something is absent, unknown or not stated (for example a smoking history or performance status the document does not give): leave that list empty.
- Never invent information. Use "" for text that is not stated, not_stated (where offered) for a choice the document does not settle, 0 for an integer that is not stated, and [] for an empty list.
- Use the choices exactly as listed. When a choice field is other, its companion text field must name the value.

DATES
- Write dates as YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY-MM or YYYY, exactly as precise as the document states them, or "" when the document gives no date. A date written without its year may take its year from the document date when that is unambiguous. Do not compute a date from a relative expression such as "two weeks ago" or "last spring".
- When an imaging report's own study, or a pathology report's own specimen, has no date written, leave its date "": the document date is applied in code.

EVIDENCE
- Every item has 1-4 evidence quotes. Each quote is copied exactly, character for character, from the document text between the document tags: the shortest span (a phrase or a sentence, at most 600 characters) that supports the item. Do not paraphrase, abbreviate, correct spelling, join separate passages, or quote the lines above the document tags.
- Quote the words that support each value you record; for an imaging study, for example, the study title or technique line, and for a procedure the specimen line.
