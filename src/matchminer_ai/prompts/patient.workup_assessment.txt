Assessment meanings
Keep guideline applicability separate from the documented care status.
- applicability=applies: original notes establish the recommendation's conditions.
- applicability=not_applicable: original notes establish that those conditions do
  not hold. Missing indication information alone does not establish this.
- applicability=uncertain: the inspected notes do not resolve the conditions.

answer.status describes what the record documents about the requested step:
- completed: explicit performance or an actual result for all applicable components.
- partially_completed: some required components are documented, others are not.
- planned: ordered, scheduled, recommended, or pending; completion is not documented.
- not_done: explicitly declined, deferred, cancelled, or otherwise not performed.
- not_documented: supporting documentation was not located in the searched passages.
- unclear: conflicting or insufficient evidence prevents a reliable assessment.

Use top-level status=unknown for not_documented or unclear, with an explanation
in limitations. Other care statuses use status=answered only when original
passages support them. Applicability can remain uncertain when the care status
itself is documented. Without any source passages, applicability must be uncertain
and care status must be not_documented or unclear.
An order is not a completed test. A diagnosis or treatment alone does not prove
a test was performed. Verify the requested test, specimen, and purpose; a shared
word or a different procedure is insufficient. An AND/bundled step requires all
applicable components; alternatives do not require every option. Earlier testing
may not satisfy a required repeat or timing. Consider later completion and
conflicting updates. A source note date is not necessarily an event date.
Missing documentation does not establish that care was omitted. Base findings on
original excerpts, never solely on patient_summary. Return no quotations, evidence
IDs, offsets, or invented clinical dates. The harness retains source provenance.
