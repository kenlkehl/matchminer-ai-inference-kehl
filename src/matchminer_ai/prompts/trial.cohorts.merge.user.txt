TASK
Group the cohorts whose eligibility criteria are essentially the same: every person who meets one cohort's criteria would meet the other's, and every person excluded from one would be excluded from the other. Return only the grouping, by cohort number; do not rewrite, summarize, or repeat any criteria.

-Compare the criteria themselves, not the cohort names or the treatments, doses, or schedules that the names mention. Cohorts that differ only in the treatment, dose, or schedule they receive belong together when their criteria are essentially the same.
-Ignore differences that do not change who is eligible: wording, order, formatting, numbering, restating the same requirement in more or less detail, or a source-limitation note about the same gap.
-Ignore differences of extraction: a criterion, note, or permission that one cohort's text states and another's leaves out is usually a shared criterion that one extraction missed. It does not keep cohorts apart unless it concerns what defines a cohort's population: its disease, histology, stage, biomarker, prior-therapy or line-of-therapy requirement, or age or sex requirement.
-Keep cohorts apart when their criteria conflict or define different populations: a different disease, histology, stage, biomarker, prior-therapy or line-of-therapy requirement, age or sex requirement, performance status, laboratory or organ-function threshold, or time window, or a population-defining criterion that only one of them has.
-When you are unsure whether cohorts define different populations, keep them apart.
-Every cohort number appears in exactly one group. A cohort without an equivalent is a group by itself.

OUTPUT FORMAT
Return only one JSON object with exactly one field, "groups": an array of groups, each an array of cohort numbers in increasing order, with the groups ordered by their first cohort number, for example {{"groups": [[1, 3], [2], [4, 5]]}}. Add no other fields, text, or Markdown fences, and end the response with the object's closing brace.
