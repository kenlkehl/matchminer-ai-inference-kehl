Enumerate each eligible histology for the parent's single primary site and age/sex group. If any histology is allowed, list all reasonably common histologies consistent with the source, one per branch. Use spelled-out morphology such as adenocarcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, invasive ductal carcinoma, acute myeloid leukemia, diffuse large B cell lymphoma, or melanoma. Do not use any, solid tumor, or an unspecified catch-all. Do not combine histologies with OR in one value.
Protein expression, receptor status and gene alterations belong in biomarkers, not histology. Preserve their applicable restrictions in scope, even when they define a subtype. Retain special subtype exceptions so a broader parent diagnosis cannot bypass their distinct treatment requirements.
