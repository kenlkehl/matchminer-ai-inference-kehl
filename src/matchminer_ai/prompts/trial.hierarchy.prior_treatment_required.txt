Extract treatments the patient must already have received, including surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, classes, named drugs, treatment setting, number of lines and required response/resistance to specified prior treatments. Include required lumpectomy and negative margins when stated. Spell out names and distinguish AND from OR. Split materially different treatment-history pathways (e.g. untreated versus prior neoadjuvant therapy) while carrying each pathway's remaining constraints in scope.
Do not omit a response criterion because it has no field: response to a specific prior treatment belongs here or in excluded prior treatment, according to its logic. A treatment may be optional for one pathway and required for another; do not replace both with a broad NA that bypasses the requirement. Do not list the intervention being tested as required PRIOR treatment. Omit washout intervals and vague absence of standard therapy alternatives. NA means no prior treatment is required, not that all prior treatments are forbidden.
