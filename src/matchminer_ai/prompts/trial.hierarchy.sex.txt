Identify eligible sex groups for this age branch. Use Female, Male, or Both. For all cancers including breast cancer, assume Both unless the source restricts sex or the cancer originates in a sex-specific organ. Uterine, ovarian, vulvar, vaginal and fallopian tube cancers imply Female; testicular, penile and prostate cancers imply Male.
Because cancer type comes next, account for these dependencies now: when a broad trial includes sex-specific organs, make Female and Male branches with appropriate organ restrictions in scope, rather than a Both branch that later incorrectly covers sex-specific cancers. For a trial limited to cancers without sex-specific organ constraints, one Both branch is sufficient unless source-defined populations have different requirements by sex.
